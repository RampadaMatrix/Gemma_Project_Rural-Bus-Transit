## 1. Overview and Description

The **Gemma 4 Good Hackathon** challenges participants to harness the power of Gemma 4 models to drive positive change and global impact. Your mission is to create a solution that addresses a real-world challenge—whether that is an application that helps millions or a specialized model that exponentially scales innovation.

With the release of Gemma 4, developers are equipped with local frontier intelligence, native function calling, and multimodal understanding. Participants are encouraged to enhance Gemma 4 models through post-training, domain adaptation, and agentic retrieval to ensure accurate, grounded outputs. 

Whether optimizing E2B and E4B models for edge-based solutions or deploying the 26B and 31B weights for complex tasks, every contribution aims to push the boundaries of AI for good.

### Key Impact Areas:
* **Health & Sciences:** Bridge the gap between humans and data to accelerate discovery or democratize knowledge.
* **Global Resilience:** Build the systems of tomorrow (e.g., offline, edge-based disaster response, climate mitigation) that anticipate and respond to pressing challenges.
* **Future of Education:** Reimagine learning by building multi-tool agents that adapt to individuals and empower educators.
* **Digital Equity & Inclusivity:** Break down barriers through linguistic diversity, intuitive interfaces, and tools that close the AI skills gap.
* **Safety & Trust:** Pioneer frameworks for transparency and reliability, ensuring AI remains grounded and explainable.

## 2. Submission Requirements

A valid submission must be completed before the deadline and include the following core components:

### A. Kaggle Writeup
The *Proof of Work*. This paper- or blog-style report (up to 1,500 words) serves as your technical verification. It must clearly explain:
* App architecture.
* How Gemma 4 was specifically utilized.
* Challenges overcome and justifications for technical choices.

### B. Attached Public Video (The Star of the Show)
The video is the most critical part of your pitch.
* **Format:** Uploaded to YouTube (publicly viewable without login).
* **Length:** Maximum of 3 minutes.
* **Content:** Tell a powerful story. Show the problem, how the Gemma 4 app solves it, and highlight the "wow" factor. 

### C. Public Code Repository
The *Source of Truth*. Provide a link to a public repository (e.g., GitHub, Kaggle Notebook).
* Must be well-documented.
* Clearly show the implementation of Gemma 4.
* Used to validate the authenticity of the project.

### D. Live Demo
A URL or files for your working demo, allowing judges to experience the project firsthand. Must be publicly accessible without a login or paywall.

### E. Media Gallery
Attach images and videos associated with your submission. A cover image is required.
